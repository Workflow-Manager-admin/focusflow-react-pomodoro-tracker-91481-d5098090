# Pomodoro App – Design Inspiration & Notes (from Reference Image)

## Visual/Creative Elements

### 1. Iconography & Graphics
- **Top tomato icon**: A friendly, hand-drawn tomato sits at the header. This sets a playful and inviting tone, appropriate for a productivity app with personality.
  - **Creative fusion idea**: Incorporate a tomato or similar illustrated mascot (static or animated) at the top of the timer, or as an 'end of session' celebration graphic.

### 2. Typography
- **Header Font**: All-caps, bold serif or playful custom font for "THE POMODORO TECHNIQUE". Slightly whimsical; not too corporate.
- **Body/Text Font**: Clean sans-serif, easy to read for the instructions and listed steps.
- **List Numbers**: Large, bold white numbers in colored circles, standing out as the most visually prominent elements after the header.

### 3. Layout & Structure
- **Single column, left-aligned**.
- **Header**: Icon + Title (“THE POMODORO TECHNIQUE”).
- **Section subheadings**: "STEPS:" and "5 REASONS TO USE…", in bold.
- **Step List**: Vertical, with clear separation and step numbers inside red circular badges, white numbering.
- **Footer bar**: Motivational text/bar at the bottom. A subtle box background is given to the "5 reasons" section.

### 4. Color Palette (Sampled & Inferred)
- **Primary Red**: (#EB5747, approx) – Used for tomato, numbered badges, and as an accent.
  - Example CSS: `--primary-accent: #EB5747;`
- **Soft Cream/Beige** background (#FFF3EA, approx) – Gentle and low-contrast, for less eye-strain and a cozy feel.
  - Example CSS: `--canvas-bg: #FFF3EA;`
- **Brownish Black** text (#2B2321, approx) – Natural instead of blue-black for a hand-written or organic feeling.
  - Example CSS: `--primary-text: #2B2321;`
- **Muted Gold/Orange** lines (#E2BC8A, approx) – Subtle section dividers.
  - Example CSS: `--divider: #E2BC8A;`
- **White** (#FFFFFF) – Used for number text on red badges.  

### 5. Spacing & Alignment
- **Moderate to generous margin** around content blocks.
- **Padding** inside sections, especially beneath headers and around lists.
- **Vertical rhythm**: Even spacing between list items (about 1.5x text height).
- **Bold visual breaks** between section headers (steps vs. reasons).

## UI Patterns & Creative Ideas to Include

### A. Themed Timer Display
- Main timer number could be inside a stylized tomato or a circular red badge echoing the step icons.
- Animated tomato mascot, e.g., small happy tomato “grows” or “wobbles” at the end of a Pomodoro.

### B. Motivational/Instruction Area
- Always-visible (or expandable) "How it works" section styled similarly to the steps list in the image.
- Rotating display of "5 reasons to use the technique" at the bottom.

### C. List/Step Styling
- Any instruction lists, completed Pomodoros, or user actions can be displayed as:
  - Numbered item
  - Red badge (circle) with white number
  - Simple, clear text

### D. Color & Mood
- Retain warmth and friendliness—use red accent, off-white canvas, and avoid harsh/clinical colors.
- All UI controls—start/stop buttons, toggles—should echo the rounded, soft shapes of the tomato and badges.

### E. Responsive Layout Suggestion
- On larger screens/tablets, consider a split view:
  - Left: Steps/instructions/motivations
  - Right: Main timer + controls

## Quick Style Guide (Synthesized)

- **Primary Typeface**: Playful serif for headings, sans-serif for body (e.g., "Quicksand", "Montserrat", or "Nunito").
- **Primary Accent Color**: #EB5747
- **Background Color**: #FFF3EA
- **Text Color**: #2B2321
- **Secondary Accent**: #E2BC8A
- **Number Badges**: Circular, filled with #EB5747, bold white text.
- **Iconography**: Hand-drawn or illustrated fruits/veg, rounded shapes only.
- **Button Style**: Rounded, filled, with accent background color.
